Create your first course

You do not have a course in the selected semester yet. A course is the workspace that holds your materials, AI Tutors, and student access settings.

Get started

  1. Choose Add in the left navigation.
  2. Select New Course and enter a short course ID and a clear title.
  3. Open Materials and upload the documents that a Tutor may use.
  4. Choose Add → Add Tutor to add and configure your first AI Tutor.
  5. Open Users when you are ready to give students access.
Semester matters: Courses are organised by semester. Check the semester selector at the top before creating the course.
