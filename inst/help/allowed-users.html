Allowed users

This page controls who may access courses managed by this teacher account. Use one row for each person.

Managing access

  1. Add a user and enter the identifier requested by your installation, usually an email address.
  2. Assign access to the appropriate course or courses.
  3. Remove users who should no longer have access.
  4. Choose Save users to apply the changes.
Check carefully: Access changes affect who can open student-facing course content. If you are unsure which identifier to enter, ask the person who administers your uLLMe installation.
