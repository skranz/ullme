Usage statistics

This page summarises how the AI Tutors in your courses are being used. It is intended to help you spot activity and technical problems without reading students' conversations.

What you can do

  • Choose a time period to focus the summary and charts.
  • Compare requests, sessions, response times, token use, and errors.
  • Use the course and Tutor breakdowns to see where activity occurred.
  • Refresh the statistics if recent sessions are not shown yet.

An empty chart normally means that no matching Tutor sessions have been recorded for the selected period.
